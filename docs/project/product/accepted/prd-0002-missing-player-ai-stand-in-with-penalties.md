# PRD-0002 — Missing Player AI Stand-In with Mimicry and Absence Costs

## Who this is for

Tabletop roleplaying parties when one player cannot attend a scheduled game session.

## What the person cannot do today

In traditional tabletop gaming, an absent player either halts the entire campaign session or forces the DM to awkwardly pilot two characters at once ("ghosting" the PC). This breaks immersion and creates scheduling nightmares.

## What good looks like

- **Autonomous Stand-In**: An AI persona takes control of the absent player's character, mimicking their personality, speech cadence, and combat style based on their character sheet.
- **Absence Penalties ("Session Miss Costs")**: The DM or group can inflict humorous or tactical penalties on the stand-in character (e.g., "Drunk", "Foolishness", "Excessive Cowardice").
- **Voice Mimicry & Roleplay**: The stand-in speaks with an appropriate persona voice, delivering flavorful dialogue reflecting the character's personality and active penalties.
- **Session Continuity**: The session proceeds without canceling; when the human player returns next week, they receive an audio and text recap of what their character did while under the influence of the penalty.

## What this does not do

- It does not permanently kill off or make irreversible story-ending choices for the absent player's character without DM confirmation.
- It does not prevent other players from helping or guarding the stand-in.
