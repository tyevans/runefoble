# US-0039 — Encounter Tension-Driven Adaptive Musical Scoring and Foley

## User Story

**As an** adventurer and streamer immersed in high-stakes tabletop combat (Marcus and Devon),  
**I want** background music and environmental foley to shift adaptively based on encounter tension,  
**So that** dramatic audio heightens the emotion of every roll without manual audio management.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Adaptive Music Stems**: Music seamlessly transitions between ambient exploration and intense combat based on turn events.
2. **Contextual Environmental Foley**: Generates ambient audio layers (rain, cave wind, tavern buzz) corresponding to map terrain.
3. **Synchronized Action SFX**: Spoken critical hits and spell detonations trigger synchronized sound effects over WebAudio within 100ms.
