# US-0019 — Ad-Hoc Ephemeral NPC Spawning and Scene-Wide Conditions

## User Story

**As a** Dungeon Master moderating intense encounters (Evelyn),  
**I want to** instantly spawn ad-hoc minion stat blocks and apply scene-wide environmental hazards,  
**So that** I can adjust combat difficulty and narrative pacing on the fly without heavy spreadsheet prep.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Instant Minion Instantiation**: Evelyn can spawn 1-hit or standard NPC/monster tokens with temporary HP directly on the canvas without full character registration.
2. **Scene-Wide Conditions**: Applying global effects (e.g. "Poison Gas", "Silence", "Blizzard") broadcasts the condition to all affected tokens and character sheets in the zone.
3. **Pacing Telemetry Alerts**: The session manager flags prolonged stall times (>3 min turn inactivity) with suggested reinforcement triggers.
