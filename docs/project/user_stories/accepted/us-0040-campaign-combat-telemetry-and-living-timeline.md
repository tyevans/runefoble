# US-0040 — Campaign Combat Telemetry and Living Interactive Timeline

## Governing PRD
- [`PRD-0012: Campaign Telemetry, Analytics & Historical Memory Archive`](../../product/accepted/prd-0012-campaign-telemetry-and-living-chronicle-timeline.md)

## User Story

**As a** returning player and content creator (Sarah and Devon),  
**I want to** explore a living interactive timeline of campaign history with combat telemetry heatmaps,  
**So that** we can celebrate memorable party achievements and analyze tactical performance across sessions.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Interactive Campaign Timeline**: Visualizes historical session chronicles, major boss defeats, and character milestones chronologically.
2. **Combat Telemetry Heatmaps**: Maps party damage taken, damage dealt, and lethal map coordinates across past encounters.
3. **Turn Performance Analytics**: Computes MVP awards, most clutch healing, and critical roll distributions per session.
