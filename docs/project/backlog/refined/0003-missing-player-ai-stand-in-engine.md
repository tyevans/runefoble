# TASK-0003: Missing Player AI Stand-In Engine with Absence Penalties

## Description
Develop the missing player AI stand-in pipeline in `services/the_watcher` and `services/character_sheet`. When a player is absent, their character is piloted by the AI engine with personality traits from their sheet and behavioral modifications driven by DM-inflicted penalties ("drunk", "foolishness").

## Governing Documents
- ADRs: ADR-0002, ADR-0007
- PRDs: PRD-0002
- User Stories: US-0002, US-0005

## Definition of Done
1. Characters marked as `is_ai_stand_in = True` automatically take turns when prompted by `services/game_session`.
2. Penalties ("drunk", "foolishness") alter combat rolls (e.g. `1d20-2` for drunk perception) and vocal dialogue generated by `services/voice_agent`.
3. Stand-in actions emit `SessionPenaltyEvent` and log to the narrative chronicle.
4. An absentee recap summary endpoint generates a humorous recap of actions for when the human player returns.
